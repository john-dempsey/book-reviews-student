Book Reviews Tutorial

  • Lesson 1: Project Setup, Schema & Authentication

    Stand up the schema and Eloquent models for the book review catalogue, add Breeze authentication, and write this lesson's first tests.

  • Lesson 2: Seeding & Browsing the Catalogue

    Real factories and seeders producing a believable demo catalogue, then read-only routes for browsing it - and the tests that prove both work.

  • Lesson 3: Full CRUD for Books

    Add create, edit, and delete to the read-only catalogue from Lesson 2 - validation and form repopulation, a shared form partial, cover image upload, and the tests that prove the whole flow works.

  • Lesson 4: Reviews

    Add the other half of 'book reviews' - writing one, editing or deleting your own, a page listing everything you've written, and the tests that prove ownership is actually enforced.
