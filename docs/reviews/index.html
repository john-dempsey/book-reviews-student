Lesson 4: Reviews

Add the other half of 'book reviews' - writing one, editing or deleting your own, a page listing everything you've written, and the tests that prove ownership is actually enforced.

  • Writing a review

    ReviewController@create and @store behind this course's first nested resource route, rating/comment validation, and turning the migration's unique index into a friendly redirect instead of a raw duplicate-entry error.

  • Editing and deleting your own review

    A flat reviews/{review} route alongside the nested one, a shared form partial, and the manual abort(403) ownership check guarding edit, update, and destroy alike - a deliberate stand-in for the Policy class Part 2 introduces.

  • My reviews

    A /my-reviews page listing everything the logged-in user has written, across every book - a plain route rather than a resource action, auth()->user()->reviews() reused the same way books.index already reuses withCount/withAvg, and latest() as a small new shorthand.

  • Testing reviews

    Feature tests for writing, editing, and deleting a review - the same happy-path/validation-failure shape lesson 3.5 used for books, plus the one thing books never needed: proving the ownership check actually returns a 403 when someone else tries.
